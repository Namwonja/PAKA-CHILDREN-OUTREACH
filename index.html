Who We Are

Our Story

"Born out of deep compassion for young lives across Kenya, Paka Children Outreach began with a simple belief: every child deserves basic dignity to thrive."

Founded in Kenya, Paka Children Outreach directly addresses the quiet hurdles that keep children out of school or lower their self-esteem. We saw students missing school due to lack of sanitary pads, walking miles barefoot on rough terrain, or sitting through lessons on an empty stomach.

Through direct community partnerships, transparent grassroots efforts, and donor generosity, we step in to ensure no child is left behind.

Our Vision

To be a beacon of hope in East Africa, fostering thriving school communities where every child, regardless of background, has equal access to basic physical needs and a dignified childhood.

Our Mission

To provide continuous essential care—decent school clothing, comfortable shoes, daily snacks, and sanitary pad hygiene packs—empowering children to stay in school and dream bigger.
